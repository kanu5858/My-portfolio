About Me

I'm a passionate mobile developer who believes in the intersection of clean code and beautiful experiences. My journey began with Android development and evolved into a holistic approach to creative problem-solving.

I specialize in crafting modern mobile applications using Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, with a deep appreciation for architecture patterns that scale. But beyond the code, I'm fascinated by the art of user experience and the psychology of intuitive design.

My philosophy is simple: write code that humans can read, build interfaces that feel natural, and never stop learning. Every project is an opportunity to push boundaries and create something meaningful.

I bring pixels to life through code, shape them through design, and give them rhythm through video.
